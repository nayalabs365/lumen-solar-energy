FAQ

A few things people ask Lumen.

Got questions? We've got clear, honest answers. If you don't see yours here, chat with Lumen anytime.

About Lumen
Is Lumen really free?
+
Totally free. Lumen's solar report, research, and recommendations cost you nothing. You'll never be charged for anything.
How does Lumen make money?
+
Installers in Lumen's network pay a fee when a homeowner chooses to move forward. This never affects what you pay for solar — and it's what keeps Lumen completely free for you.
How is Lumen different from "get 3 solar quotes" sites?
+
Those sites sell your info the second you hit submit — sometimes to half a dozen companies at once. Lumen does the opposite. It keeps your info private, does all the research for you, and only introduces you to one recommended installer when you decide you're ready.
Where does Lumen operate?
+
Lumen is currently helping homeowners in Illinois, with more states coming soon.
Your Privacy
Will Lumen call me?
+
Yes — but not to sell you anything. After you request your report, Lumen reaches out personally to confirm your details, walk you through your results, and answer any questions. Think of it as a welcome call from your concierge, not a sales pitch. Beyond that, your contact information is never sold or shared with any other company.
Will I get bombarded with calls from solar companies?
+
Never. That's Lumen's whole reason for existing. Your contact information stays with Lumen and is never shared with any installer unless you explicitly approve it. The only time an installer hears from you is when you tell Lumen you're ready to talk to someone.
Can I browse installers without sharing my info?
+
Absolutely. You can search and browse Lumen's installer network freely. No information is shared until you click "Let Lumen connect me" and confirm you're ready.
Your Solar Report
How accurate is the report Lumen sends me?
+
Lumen uses real satellite imagery, local utility rates, and current incentive programs to build your estimate. It's a strong starting point — your installer will refine it with a detailed site visit.
What data goes into my report?
+
Your report is built using satellite roof analysis, local sunlight data, your utility provider's rates, your average energy usage, and all currently available federal, state, and local solar incentives in your area.
How long does it take to get my report?
+
Your initial report is generated within minutes of submitting your address. Lumen will then reach out to walk you through the details within 24 hours.
Going Solar
What if I'm not ready yet?
+
No rush. Your report is yours to keep. Lumen doesn't pressure, follow up aggressively, or expire your information. Whenever you're ready, Lumen's here.
Do I have to use Lumen's recommended installer?
+
Not at all. Lumen's recommendation is just that — a recommendation. You're free to shop around or go with any installer you choose. The report and research are yours regardless.
What payment options are available for solar?
+
Your report covers three main options: paying cash (highest long-term savings), taking a solar loan (low monthly payments, no money down), or leasing/PPA (lowest upfront commitment). Your installer can walk you through the details of each.
