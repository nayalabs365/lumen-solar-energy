How It Works

Here's what Lumen does for you.

Three simple steps. Zero pressure. Total transparency. Lumen handles the research, protects your privacy, and only connects you when you're ready.

01

Lumen gets to know your home

Tell Lumen your address and a bit about your energy usage. From there, it uses AI to analyze your roof orientation, local sunlight hours, utility rates, and every available rebate and incentive to build a solar report that's specific to your home.

🏠
Satellite roof analysis for optimal panel placement
☀️
Local sunlight data and seasonal production estimates
💰
Current utility rates and available state/federal incentives
📊
Personalized savings projections over 25 years
02

Lumen shops around so you don't have to

While you go about your day, Lumen quietly researches vetted installers in your area, compares pricing and options, and narrows it down to the best fit for your situation. No one contacts you during this process. No one even knows you're looking.

🔍
Compares multiple local installers behind the scenes
🛡️
Your info stays locked down — no one knows you're shopping
⭐
Evaluates licensing, reviews, workmanship, and pricing
✅
Identifies the best match for your home and budget
03

Lumen brings you a recommendation

When Lumen has found the right match, it walks you through the recommendation personally — who, why, and what to expect. You review everything on your own time. If you want to move forward, Lumen sets up the introduction. If not, no worries.

💬
Lumen calls to walk you through results — a concierge call, not a sales call
📋
Clear recommendation with reasoning you can review
🤝
Introduction only happens when you say you're ready
⏰
No deadline, no pressure — Lumen moves at your pace
