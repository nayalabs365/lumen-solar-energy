About Lumen

Solar shopping should be simple.

Lumen Solar Concierge was built because we believe every homeowner deserves honest, unbiased guidance when going solar — without the sales pressure.

Our Mission

We're on your side. That's the whole idea.

Most solar companies want to sell you their panels. Lumen isn't a solar company. We're a concierge — an independent service that works exclusively for the homeowner.

We use AI to analyze your home, research local installers, and present you with clear, unbiased recommendations. Your information stays private until you choose to share it. No cold calls. No bidding wars. No pressure.

We believe that when homeowners have the right information and the right support, they make great decisions. Lumen is that support.
