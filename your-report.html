Your Solar Report

Let Lumen take a look at your home.

Enter a few details and Lumen will build a personalized solar report — complete with production estimates, savings projections, and available incentives.

What's in your report?

Your Lumen solar report is built using real satellite data, local utility rates, and current incentive programs. It's not a one-size-fits-all estimate — it's specific to your roof, your energy usage, and your area.

Your report includes

📊Recommended system size based on your roof and energy usage
☀️Monthly and annual production estimates
💰25-year savings projection
🏛️Federal tax credit + state and local incentives you qualify for
📋Side-by-side comparison of cash, loan, and lease options
🌱Environmental impact — CO₂ offset, trees planted equivalent

What happens next?

After you submit your details, Lumen generates your report and sends it to you. Then Lumen personally reaches out to walk you through the results, answer questions, and make sure everything makes sense. It's a concierge call — not a sales pitch.

From there, Lumen quietly researches local installers and brings you a recommendation when it finds the right match. Your info stays private the entire time.

How accurate is it?

Very. Lumen uses real satellite imagery, PVWatts solar modeling, live utility rate data, and up-to-date incentive databases. The report is a strong starting point — your installer will refine the final numbers during a detailed site assessment.
